### Key Features
1. **Wall painter establishes ceiling line** - Critical difference from ceiling finish spec. Ceiling line cut-in is the defining edge task for wall finish.
2. **Trim masking ownership** - This spec owns the trim masking task. When painting finish on drywall walls, ALL trim (baseboard, door casing, window casing, crown molding, chair rail, shadow boxes, etc.) is masked and cut in.
3. **Wall-specific production rates** - Rates are ~15-20% faster than ceiling equivalents due to no overhead fatigue.
4. **Spray/backroll coupling** - Spray rate correctly constrained to backroll rate per doctrine.
5. **QT-scaled coat counts** - QT2=1 coat, QT3=2 coats (1 acceptable), QT4-5=2 coats mandatory.
6. **Sheen/QT gates** - Flat/matte/eggshell at any QT, satin at QT4+, semi-gloss at QT5 only, gloss NOT available.
