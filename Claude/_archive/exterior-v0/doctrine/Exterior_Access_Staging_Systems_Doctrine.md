## 1. Access Method Selection Matrix

| Equipment Type | Optimal Height Range | Typical Application | Setup Time | Best For |
|---------------|---------------------|---------------------|-----------|----------|
| **Extension Ladder** | 0-28 ft | Spot repairs, trim, 1-story siding | 1-5 min | Speed, portability, surgical strikes |
| **Pump Jack System** | 10-48 ft | Multi-story siding, full repaints | 1.5-3 hrs per facade | Production siding on 2-3 story |
| **Sectional Scaffold** | 0-125+ ft | Masonry, historic restoration, long-duration work | 3+ hrs | Maximum stability, multiple workers |
| **Aerial Lift** | 20-60+ ft | Gable ends, steep rooflines, speed | Drive & operate | Extreme heights, speed |

### When to Use Each

- **Extension ladders**: Single-story structures; "surgical strikes" on multi-story (fascia, soffits, isolated trim). Rapid deployment by single operator. High repositioning overhead on large surfaces.
- **Pump jacks**: Preferred for full-scale siding repaints on 2-3 story. 24-ft lateral reach per setup. Adjustable height for ergonomic optimization. Use when >500 SF of 2nd-story siding.
- **Sectional scaffolding**: Heavy restoration, masonry, multiple workers needed simultaneously, long-duration elevated work. Historic landmarks.
- **Aerial lifts**: High-rise residential, gable ends. Constrained by soft turf, narrow yards, underground septic. Rental economics favor occasional use.

## 2. Pump Jack Setup and Teardown Times

### Two-Story Residential — Per Facade (2-Person Crew)

| Task Component | Setup Duration | Teardown Duration |
|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Foundation / pole prep | 30-45 min | 15-20 min |
| Bracing & jack install | 45-60 min | 20-30 min |
| Platform & railings | 30-45 min | 25-40 min |
| **Total per facade** | **105-150 min (1.75-2.5 hrs)** | **60-90 min (1-1.5 hrs)** |

### Key Setup Requirements
- **Foundation check**: If screwdriver pushes 6" into soil by hand, mudsills/baseplates mandatory
- **Pole heights**: Available in 6, 12, 18, 24 ft increments; joined with connectors up to 50 ft
- **Bracing**: Anchored to building with roof/folding braces. Heights >24 ft need intermediate braces every 10-15 ft
- **Platform**: OSHA minimum 12" wide; preferred 14-24"
- **Guardrails**: Mandatory on all open sides for platforms >= 10 ft

### Estimation Rule
Setup/teardown is **non-billable overhead** — must be a distinct line item. Failure to account = 10-15% profit margin erosion.

## 3. Production Rate Degradation by Height

### Height Tier Multipliers

| Story Count | Labor Hours Multiplier | Cost Increase | Typical Timeline (2,000 SF home, 3-person crew) |
|------------|----------------------|---------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **1-Story** | 1.00 (base) | 0% | 2-3 days |
| **2-Story** | 1.15-1.25 | +15-25% | 3-5 days |
| **3-Story** | 1.35-1.50+ | +35-50%+ | 5-7 days |

### Production Rates by Substrate and Access Method

| Substrate Type | 1-Story Rate (SF/hr) | 2-Story w/ Ladders | 2-Story w/ Pump Jacks |
|---------------|---------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Smooth siding | 125 | 85 | 105 |
| Stucco / brick | 100 | 65 | 80 |
| Intricate trim | 40 | 25 | 35 |

**Key insight**: Pump jacks "recover" a significant portion of the height penalty by mimicking ground-level ergonomics. At 2-story:
- Ladders: ~32% rate reduction on smooth siding
- Pump jacks: ~16% rate reduction on smooth siding

### Rate Calculation Formula
```
Rate_Adjusted = Rate_Base x (1 - Degradation_Factor)
```

## 5. Productivity: Pump Jacks vs. Ladders vs. Scaffolding

### Comparison Matrix

| Factor | Extension Ladders | Pump Jacks | Sectional Scaffold | Aerial Lift |
|--------|------------------|------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Setup time** | 1-5 min | 1.5-3 hrs/facade | 3+ hrs | Drive & operate |
| **Repositioning** | Every 4-6 ft lateral | Every 24 ft lateral | Fixed in place | Continuous |
| **Non-productive time** | High (constant repositioning) | Low (24 ft lateral reach) | Very low | Very low |
| **Worker fatigue** | High (rung ergonomics) | Low (platform, adjustable height) | Low (full platform) | Low |
| **2-story rate recovery** | 0% (baseline degradation) | ~50% of height penalty recovered | ~60% recovered | ~70% recovered |
| **Multi-worker** | No | Yes (2 workers) | Yes (multiple) | No (1-2) |
| **Narrow yards** | Difficult (<8 ft) | Excellent (vertical footprint) | Moderate | Poor |

### Key Advantage of Pump Jacks
- 24-ft aluminum stage = massive horizontal coverage
- Two-person crew covers entire facade section without descending
- Worker adjusts platform to optimal ergonomic height
- Significantly reduces the "height modifier" fatigue factor

## 6. Access Constraint Modifiers

| Site Constraint | Labor Impact | Preferred Access Method |
|----------------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Narrow side yard (< 8 ft)** | +25% setup time | Pump jack (vertical footprint advantage) |
| **Steep grade (> 15 degrees)** | +40% setup time | Sectional scaffold with adjustable base jacks |
| **Dense landscaping** | +15% setup time | Aerial lift or extension poles |
| **Soft/wet ground** | +20% setup time | Pump jack with mudsills/baseplates |

### Additional Constraints
- Underground septic/utilities: Precludes heavy aerial lifts
- Overhead power lines: Requires minimum clearance distances
- Grade changes: Adjustable base jacks essential; improper leveling is primary cause of tip-over accidents
- Homeowner access: Must maintain safe pathways during multi-day projects

## Total Project Labor Formula

```
TPL = (A x R_base x M_h) + (P_hours x M_s) + S_hours
```

Where:
- `A` = Surface area (SF)
- `R_base` = Base production rate (hrs/SF)
- `M_h` = Height modifier (1.0 to 1.75)
- `P_hours` = Prep hours
- `M_s` = Surface condition multiplier (from Topic 1 DSD table)
- `S_hours` = Setup/teardown hours (distinct line item)

### Example: 2,000 SF 2-Story Home (6-Day Schedule)

| Day | Activity | Hours (3-person crew) |
|-----|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | Washing + initial setup | 6-8 hrs |
| 2 | Prep and repairs | 6-8 hrs |
| 3 | Priming | 6-8 hrs |
| 4-5 | Painting (2 coats) | 12-16 hrs |
| 6 | Touch-ups + teardown | 4-6 hrs |

### Access Constraint Modifiers
- `MOD_ACCESS_NARROW_YARD` = +25%
- `MOD_ACCESS_STEEP_GRADE` = +40%
- `MOD_ACCESS_DENSE_LANDSCAPE` = +15%
- `MOD_ACCESS_SOFT_GROUND` = +20%

### Setup/Teardown as Line Items
- Pump jack setup: 1.75-2.5 hrs per facade (2-person crew)
- Pump jack teardown: 1.0-1.5 hrs per facade (2-person crew)
- Must be estimated separately from production rates — not embedded in SF rates

### Equipment Selection Logic
- 1-story: ladders (default)
- 2-story with >500 SF elevated siding: pump jacks
- 3-story: pump jacks or sectional scaffold
- Gable ends / extreme height: aerial lift rental
- Narrow yard (<8 ft): pump jacks (vertical footprint)
- Steep grade (>15 deg): sectional scaffold with adjustable jacks
