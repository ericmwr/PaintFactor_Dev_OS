**Trim & Molding Protection Matrix**

*Spray application — substrate protection & masking requirements*✅  ❌

Anytime spraying is present in room full room gets masked OR Containment must be built.  
Containment \- Floor cover \+ 4 Zip Poles with visqueen plastic barrier walls or full floor cover and walls draped in room.    
  Authority is User Selected Masked Hardwood.  Backup Authority, Flooring installed in room, any spray finish or spray and backroll selection is made for any substrate.  Backup Authority, Room has flooring installed any ceiling painting is being done \- mask full floor.  Backup Authority, Walls without ceilings being painted

Three Levels of Masking \-   
Edge Only \- Brush and roll of adjoining surface to protect or create tape line edge. 

Partial Cover \- Drape / cover.  Pin plastic curtain on vertical surfaces, drape drop clothes or plastic on horizontal flat surfaces When painting overhead to protext from roller splatter, brush drips when brush and roll or overspray and roller splatter when spray and backroll, or overspray when spray only. 

Full Mask \- Tape all edges, encapsulate adjoining surface with tight taped edges with all seams taped.  Typically when spraying right next to something.   overspray and roller splatter when spray and backroll, or overspray when spray only.

Each trim element will have a minimum masking requirement for protection of adjacent surfaces.  This is not the same as the tapeline edge applied after painting of trim. 

Trim elements should have option for tapeline edge where meeting wall.  If selected, the LF that adjoins wall will have tape line edge installed.  

There should be an option at project level creation for full trim tapeline edge to pre populate trim detail panels tapeline edge trigger / toggle.

(fixtures like cabinets, showers/tubs, fireplaces, builtins, benches will have their own protection toggles, don't apply here)

When masking adjoining surface there are 2 purposes.  Tape line edge and then Protection.  Generally there should be a tapeline edge applied before plastic is installed.  This promotes a straight edge.  Then field protection using plastic or paper follows over top of tapeline edge.  This means 2 layers of tape at edge.  Important for understanding labor and material usage.  

**Four Levels of Masking** \- (Need to update fields in UI)  
Edge Only \- Brush and roll of adjoining surface to protect gravity drips or splatter or create tape line edge. 

Partial Cover \- Dropclothes, Plastic, Paper.  Dropclothes on flat ground surfaces, Plastic / Paper on overhead or vertical surfaces.  Extended out from wall partially, trim element or ceiling.  For limited overhead work applications (perimeter of floor for vertical surfaces, Wall below trim.  Gravity Covering to protect from drips or splatter

Full Cover \- Cover field loosely but completely.  Draped Curtains or Cover.  For overhead work applications

Encapsulation of surface. \- Full edge tape and field seal with plastic or paper taped at seams. For Spray Applications

**Masking Panel in Room Identity** \- Enter masking requirements.  Need full Protection Module series.    
**Mask Ceiling** \-   Edge \= Tape only. Partial \= Tape line and paper or plastic extending out from wall into ceiling field on 1-3 wall edges.  Full \= Tape line around perimeter with paper or plastic extending out from wall into ceiling field  
Encapsulation \- Entire masking of surface area of ceiling.  Done in sections to prevent sagging in middle

**Mask Walls or Wall** \-  Select qty of walls to mask in room 1-4 (Respects Wall additions and deductions for room on structure tab), select level of mask Edge/Partial/Full.  Edge only → Select edge case \- Ceiling, Adjoining wall or walls, Trim Element .   
Partial → Edge → select edge case \- Ceiling, Adjoining Wall or walls, trim element, Extend out from edge case with masking paper or plastic  
Full → Drape full curtain on wall taped at ceiling  
Encapsulate → Taped at all 4 edges and fully covered tightly with plastic. For accent walls, feature walls. 

**Mask Floor** \- Edge → Tape Edge only at baseboard and door casing and door frames.  Partial \- Select qty of walls 1-3 to account for or full perimeter (respects wall additions and deductions for room on structure tab.) Dropclothes at perimeter of room or rosin paper taped down  
Full → Full floor covering with drop clothes  
Encapsulate → Full floor covering with rosin or plastic taped at all edges and at every seem to fully protect flooring.
