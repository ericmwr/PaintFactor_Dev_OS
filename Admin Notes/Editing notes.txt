Paintscope is going to be a estimators tool for doing jobsite walk downs.  It is intended to be used on a mobile device or laptop.  The idea is the UI/UX will need to capture all the project data along with images.  It will go about the same workflow as a typical painting contractor estimator.  Here is the process for estimating a painting project. 

Generally the phone call comes in for request for estimate.  We schedule the estimate and meet with the customer on site. Walk in, generally some small talk, ask some questions about the job.  Find out what it is they are looking to have done. This includes getting instruction from them on what it is that they want painted, the basic color scheme they're looking for.  We dont always have colors at this point. Customers usually want to hire a contractor first and then start a color consultation or start thinking about it after the estimate .  Take a mental note of the scope of the job.   Then once formalities are out of the way the estimator begins measuring.  For years i used a measuring wheel to measure out each room.  But over the years it became obvious that exact measurements didnt really matter much.  I started pacing out rooms and its really no different.  Even when measuring with a wheel we pad the measure by a foot, rounding up on inches.  This is a pdca standard.  
So we go room to room.  Usually writing a room name, then starting with the length and width.  Generally the ceilings are all the same level throughout unless its a room exception. So we write the ceiling height once typically unless theres a room that has a higher ceiling in which case we write that ceiling height in.  I've got the perimiter of the room with the length and width and the square of the walls with the ceiling height and the ceiling and floor.  If theres crown, chairrail, wainscote i've got all that info.  Some cases there is a specialty wall that has an accent feature in which i call out in under that room header with details of the accent.  If there is a closet ill typically write down the length and width, but honestly its always an eyeball measurement because closets have pretty standard sizes.  small medium and large.  

When it comes to windows and doors we either keep a tally somewhere on the bid sheet as we go through the house or do them after all the rooms have been recorded.  Usually best to do it after rooms so none get missed.
